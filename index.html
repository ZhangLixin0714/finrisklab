FinRiskLab

Independent research in financial risk modeling, regulatory technology (RegTech) and explainable AI for finance.

About

FinRiskLab is a personal research initiative exploring the intersection of financial risk management, AI and regulatory compliance. Led by an experienced risk manager and researcher, our goal is to share insights, methodologies and open-source prototypes that advance responsible innovation in financial services.

We are not a commercial service provider and do not offer paid consultancy. All materials shared here are for academic and experimental use.

Research & Projects

Our work covers a range of topics including risk assessment for consumer loans, anti-fraud analytics, credit risk concentration and network anomaly detection. We share code and data sets as research prototypes to foster collaboration and discussion in the community.

You can request access to our Risk Monitoring Rules Research Package via the form on our GitHub Pages site. Once your request is submitted, you'll receive a link to download the research package.
